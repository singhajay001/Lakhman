# tile-cocktails.jpg

**Class:** Homepage category tile · **Output:** 2048 × 2560 (4:5) · **Safe zone:** centre third

## Creative intent
Technique as a category. Tools only — this tile deliberately carries **no
prepared drink at all**, which keeps the highest-risk category on the safest
possible footing while still reading unmistakably as cocktails.

## Composition
Vertical frame on dark walnut. Top edge: a cut-crystal mixing glass entering
from above, cropped at its midpoint, with a bar spoon resting inside. Bottom
edge: a jigger and channel knife lying flat, cropped, with one fresh lemon peel
curl. **Middle third is bare unlit walnut.**

## Subject & set
Heavy cut-crystal mixing glass with a spent ice bed, cropped top. Long twisted-
stem bar spoon. Weighted brushed-steel jigger and channel knife, cropped bottom.
One lemon peel curl. Dark walnut with grain and a single ring mark. No coupe, no
serve, no liquid served.

## Lighting
Narrow key from camera-right catching crystal facets at the top and steel
specular at the bottom; centre band unlit and matte.
