# tile-gin.jpg

**Class:** Homepage category tile · **Output:** 2048 × 2560 (4:5) · **Safe zone:** centre third

## Creative intent
Botanical, immediately. Must not read as *whisky in a different colour* — the
distinguishing signal is scattered dry botanicals and clear glass.

## Composition
Vertical frame, overhead on honed dark stone. A drift of botanicals enters from
the top edge and thins as it descends. From the bottom edge, the base and lower
body of one unlabelled clear bottle rises, cropped, throwing a long shadow. The
**middle third is bare shadowed stone**.

## Subject & set
Juniper berries with bloom, coriander seed, a curl of lemon peel, one cassia
quill, fine spice dust — all clustered along the top edge. Unlabelled clear
bottle base with clear spirit, cropped at the bottom. Honed dark stone with
chips and water marks.

## Lighting
Hard low rake from camera-right at shallow elevation for the top cluster; a
separate low kick catching the bottle base at the bottom. Centre band flagged
off.

## Prompt
```
A tall vertical frame photographed overhead onto honed dark stone. A drift of
gin botanicals enters from the very top edge and thins as it descends — juniper
berries with waxy bloom, coriander seed, a curl of lemon peel showing white
pith, one cassia quill and a fine dust of ground spice. From the very bottom
edge, the base and lower body of one unlabelled clear glass bottle of clear
spirit rises, cropped, casting a long shadow across the stone; the bottle is
completely bare with no label. The entire middle third of the frame is bare
shadowed stone, empty and low contrast, with nothing in it. Hard low raking
light from the right gives every berry a long individual shadow at the top and
kicks the bottle base at the bottom, leaving the centre band dark. 100mm macro
at f/2.8.
```
_+ STYLE block_

## Negative
_NEGATIVE block_ · plus: `bright green leaves, fresh flowers, cucumber, tonic, ice, serving glass, centred subject, object in centre of frame, bright middle`

## Compliance
Centre band verified empty · botanicals earthy, never bright green · no serve.
