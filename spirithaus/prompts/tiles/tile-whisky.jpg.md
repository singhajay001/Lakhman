# tile-whisky.jpg

**Class:** Homepage category tile · **Output:** 2048 × 2560 (4:5) · **Safe zone:** centre third

> **Safe zone definition for tiles:** the middle third of the frame height, full
> width, must stay empty and low-contrast — a quiet horizontal band for the
> category label. Visual interest lives at the top and bottom edges and bleeds
> off them.

## Creative intent
A category doorway, not a picture. It should be legible as *whisky* at
200px wide from the top and bottom edges alone.

## Composition
Vertical frame. Upper edge: the underside of an oak cask stave and iron hoop
entering from the top, cropped hard. Lower edge: the shoulder and neck of one
unlabelled amber bottle rising from the bottom, cropped at the shoulder, with
oak shavings on dark slate. The **middle third is empty air and unlit slate** at
roughly 10–14 IRE.

## Subject & set
Charred oak stave with hoop, cropped top. Unlabelled bottle shoulder in amber
glass, cropped bottom. Dark slate. Two or three oak shavings near the lower
edge. No glass, no serve.

## Lighting
Two narrow raking keys — one from upper camera-right grazing the stave, one from
lower camera-right catching the bottle shoulder — with the centre band
deliberately unlit. This split-light structure is what creates the safe zone.
