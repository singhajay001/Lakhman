# hero-vodka.jpg

**Class:** Category hero · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
Purity through process — grain, filtration, cold. The one frame permitted to run
cool, but it must stay inside the house palette: cold is expressed through
*frost and steel*, never through blue.

## Composition
Camera at bottle shoulder height, close. A frosted unlabelled bottle stands
right of centre on a brushed steel surface, condensation running in real
tracks. Behind it, out of focus, a scatter of raw grain. Left third: unlit steel
receding to black.

## Subject & set
Unlabelled clear glass bottle wearing genuine freezer frost with finger-drag
clearings and beaded meltwater running downward. Brushed steel bench with
anisotropic streak, real fingerprints, faint scratches. A shallow spill of raw
wheat and rye grain, warm-toned, sitting behind at the edge of focus. No glass,
no serve.

## Lighting
Key from camera-right and behind at ~110°, hard and narrow, so the frost reads
as a bright halo along the bottle's right edge and the steel returns one long
directional streak. Key stays 3200K tungsten — the *coolness* comes from the
frost's neutral white and the steel, never from a blue gel.
