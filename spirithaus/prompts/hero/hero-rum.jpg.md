# hero-rum.jpg

**Class:** Category hero · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
Cane, molasses and tropical hardwood — rum as a dark, slow, sugar-derived craft
spirit. Explicitly **not** a beach, a boat, or a holiday.

## Composition
Camera slightly above bench height, three-quarter. Cut cane billets stack from
the right edge inward, ends toward camera. One unlabelled bottle of dark rum
stands right of centre among them. A shallow dish of molasses sits low in the
frame catching a single specular. Left third: unlit hardwood and air.

## Subject & set
Split sugar cane billets showing fibrous pale interior against dark rind. A
shallow black dish of thick molasses with a slow viscous ridge. Unlabelled
bottle in dark glass, dark spirit, heavy shoulder. Bench of dark tropical
hardwood with deep grain and old ring stains. Optional `#CF1C29` accent as a
single red wax seal on the bottle neck.

## Lighting
Key from camera-right and above at ~80°, moderately hard. The molasses surface
returns one long liquid specular — the frame's brightest note. Cane fibre catches
raking texture. Deep falloff to camera-left.
