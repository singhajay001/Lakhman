# hero-liqueurs.jpg

**Class:** Category hero · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
Viscosity, depth, and flavour provenance. Liqueurs as a confectioner's craft —
sugar, bean, pod and bark — rendered dark and serious rather than sweet and
bright.

## Composition
Close, camera slightly above a dark stone counter. An unlabelled bottle of thick
dark liqueur stands at the right, its shoulder catching key. Ingredient
provenance groups right of centre. One slow pour ribbon is *implied* by a
viscous residue trail on the stone, not by an active pour. Left third: bare
shadowed stone.

## Subject & set
Whole roasted coffee beans with visible oil sheen. Cracked cacao nibs. A split
vanilla pod showing seed. A shard of cassia bark. A shallow pool of dark
viscous liqueur on the stone with a thick, slow-moving edge and one bright
specular. Unlabelled dark amber glass bottle, heavy, with a plain cork.

## Lighting
Key from camera-right at ~100°, medium-hard, low enough to graze the stone so
the viscous pool returns a single elongated specular and the coffee bean oil
glints. Deep shadow left.
