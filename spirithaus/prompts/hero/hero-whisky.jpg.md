# hero-whisky.jpg

**Class:** Category hero · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
Maturation and heritage. The story is oak, time, and colour — the whisky as the
product of a cask, not the contents of a glass.

## Composition
Camera low, near bench height. A cask head fills the right half at a slight
angle, its stave joints and iron hoop reading as structure. One unlabelled
bottle stands just right of centre against it. Left third: unlit air and the
shadow side of the cask.

## Subject & set
Charred American oak cask head with visible char, raised grain and a rusted
iron hoop. One squat unlabelled bottle of deep amber spirit. A single nosing
glass, **filled to a shallow 20mm and untouched**, sitting on dark slate. Loose
oak shavings. No pourer, no ice, no second glass.

## Lighting
Key from camera-right at 90°, low, so it transmits *through* the amber spirit —
the glass and bottle glow from within against a black field. That transmitted
amber is the hero highlight of the frame.

## Prompt
```
A charred oak cask head filling the right half of the frame at a slight angle,
```

## Compliance
