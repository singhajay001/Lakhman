# hero-cocktails.jpg

**Class:** Category hero · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
The craft of preparation. Tools, precision, and technique — the reason to buy
the ingredients, not an invitation to drink the result. Highest-risk frame in
the set for compliance; hold the line.

## Composition
Camera at bench height, near-level. Bar tools lie in a considered row across the
right two-thirds of a dark walnut bar top: mixing glass at the right, jigger,
bar spoon, channel knife. **At most one** prepared drink — a coupe holding a
clear stirred serve with a single citrus twist — sits right of centre, untouched.
Left third: bare unlit walnut.

## Subject & set
Heavy cut-crystal mixing glass with a spent ice bed and residual chill haze. A
weighted brushed-steel jigger. A long twisted-stem bar spoon. A channel knife
with one fresh curl of lemon peel beside it. One coupe, filled to a correct
level, still, with a fine oil sheen on the surface. Dark walnut bar top with
grain and one honest ring mark.

## Lighting
Key from camera-right at ~95°, medium-hard. Steel tools return crisp specular
lines; the coupe's stem and meniscus carry a bright edge against black. Amber
practical far background, heavily under key.
