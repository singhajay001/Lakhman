# hero-home.jpg

**Class:** Hero banner · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
The flagship frame. It must say *specialist shop* in under a second — a curated
wall of spirits seen the way a buyer sees it, not the way a customer at a bar
sees it. Depth, selection, expertise. No occasion, no invitation.

## Composition
Camera at standing eye height, square to a backbar shelf run. The shelving
begins right of centre and recedes to the right edge, so the **left third falls
into unlit depth** — a dark, empty, uncluttered field for the headline. One
bottle at roughly the 2/3 vertical is the sharp plane; everything nearer and
further surrenders to bokeh. Horizon of the shelf line sits on the lower third.

## Subject & set
Eight to twelve unlabelled bottles of varied silhouette — squat whisky, tall
gin, shouldered rum — in clear, smoke, and amber glass, standing on a burnished
oak shelf with visible end-grain and a scuffed front edge. Behind them, dark
stained timber panelling. A single brushed-steel shelf bracket catches an edge
of key. One small `#CF1C29` element only: a plain uninked red neck band on the
sharp bottle.

## Lighting
House standard. Key rakes from camera-right at ~100°, so the bottle shoulders
carry bright rim lines against the panelling and the shelf run falls off to near
black toward camera-left. One warm amber practical deep in the background, well
under key.
