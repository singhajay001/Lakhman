# hero-gin.jpg

**Class:** Category hero · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
Botanical craft. Gin sold as a formulation — the botanical bill laid out with
the seriousness of a laboratory bench, not a garnish tray.

## Composition
Overhead-adjacent, camera ~25° off vertical onto a honed dark stone slab.
Botanicals are grouped in loose piles across the **right two-thirds**, with the
unlabelled clear bottle standing upright at the right, its base throwing a long
shadow left. The left third is bare stone in shadow.

## Subject & set
Juniper berries with visible bloom, coriander seed, angelica root, cassia bark,
a curled strip of lemon peel with pith showing, a single split cardamom pod.
One unlabelled clear glass bottle, empty of any marking, holding clear spirit.
Fine dust of ground spice. Stone shows honest chips and water marks.

## Lighting
Key hard from camera-right at a shallow 20° elevation — each berry and seed
throws a long individual shadow, and the raking angle makes the botanical
texture the subject. Clear spirit reads as a bright refracted core.

## Prompt
```
Gin botanicals arranged in loose groups across a honed dark stone slab,
photographed from a steep three-quarter overhead angle. Juniper berries with
visible waxy bloom, coriander seed, angelica root, cassia bark quills, a curled
strip of lemon peel showing white pith, one split cardamom pod, and a fine dust
of ground spice. One unlabelled clear glass bottle of clear spirit stands
upright at the right of frame, completely bare with no label and no markings,
casting a long shadow to the left. Hard low raking side light from the right at
a shallow angle makes every berry and seed throw its own long shadow and reveals
the texture of the botanicals and the chipped, water-marked stone. The entire
left third is bare shadowed stone, empty and quiet. 100mm macro at f/2.8.
```
_+ STYLE block_

## Negative
_NEGATIVE block_ · plus: `serving glass, gin and tonic, ice, tonic bottle, cucumber slice garnish, bright green foliage, fresh flowers, object in left third`

## Compliance
No serve, no garnish styling, no second bottle · botanicals stay earthy, never
bright green.
