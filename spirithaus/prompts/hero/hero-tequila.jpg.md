# hero-tequila.jpg

**Class:** Category hero · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
Agave and volcanic earth. Tequila positioned as an agricultural, terroir-driven
spirit — the antithesis of the shot-glass cliché.

## Composition
Low camera, near ground level. A cut agave piña section occupies the right half,
fibrous face toward the light. One unlabelled bottle of pale straw spirit stands
behind and right of it, partially occluded. Foreground: dark volcanic gravel
falling out of focus. Left third: unlit gravel and air.

## Subject & set
Quartered agave piña with wet, fibrous, honey-coloured cut face. Two or three
blue-grey agave leaf spears with visible fibre and a dried tip, laid low and
angled right. Rough black volcanic stone. Unlabelled bottle in clear glass with
heavy base, holding pale straw spirit.

## Lighting
Key from camera-right and behind at ~120°, hard, so the wet piña face flares
with specular moisture and the agave leaf edges carry a bright rim. Everything
camera-left is negative-filled to black. This is the most rim-driven frame in
the set.

shadow. 50mm lens at f/1.8, low angle.
