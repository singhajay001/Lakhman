# hero-home-alt.jpg

**Class:** Hero banner (A/B alternate) · **Output:** 3840 × 1600 (2.4:1) · **Safe zone:** left third

## Creative intent
The fulfilment counterpart to `hero-home`. Where the primary hero sells
*selection*, this sells *care* — the bottle handled as an object worth
protecting. Reads as a specialist despatching an order, never as a gift moment.

## Composition
Three-quarter overhead, camera ~35° off vertical, looking down onto a worn
timber packing bench that runs across the lower two-thirds. The open box and
bottle sit **right of centre**; the bench surface toward camera-left is bare,
shadowed and unlit — the headline field. Diagonal of the bench edge leads the
eye right.

## Subject & set
One unlabelled amber-glass bottle lying at a slight angle in a plain unbranded
kraft box, cradled in cream tissue with genuine crease structure. Beside it: a
coil of natural jute twine, a bare steel box cutter, a torn tissue edge. No
tape, no printing, no stamps. Optional single `#CF1C29` accent as one strand of
red cotton tape.

## Lighting
Key from upper camera-right, hard-ish, throwing the box's shadow left across the
bench and swallowing the safe zone. Amber practical off frame-right.

## Prompt
