# life-sydney.jpg

**Class:** Lifestyle / provenance · **Output:** 4096 × 2731 (3:2) · **Safe zone:** none mandated; keep right third quiet

## Creative intent
Anchors SPIRITHAUS to Sydney without a postcard. The signal is **inner-city
Sydney fabric** — honey sandstone, dark face brick, cast-iron lacework, a narrow
laneway shopfront at dusk. No harbour, no bridge, no opera house, no beach, no
vehicles.

## Composition
Camera across a narrow laneway, slightly low. A specialist shopfront occupies the
left two-thirds: dark timber frame, deep-set window, warm interior glow spilling
onto wet bluestone. Sandstone and cast-iron lacework above. The right third holds
quiet unlit brickwork.

## Subject & set
Honey-toned Sydney sandstone course above a dark-painted timber shopfront.
Victorian cast-iron lacework on a first-floor balcony. Wet bluestone paving
returning the interior glow. Inside the window, unlabelled bottles on an oak
shelf, softly out of focus. **All signage blank** — an unlettered dark fascia
board. One `#CF1C29` accent: a small plain red-painted panel beside the door.
No people, no vehicles, no bicycles.

## Lighting
Dusk. Ambient at the top of the frame is a deep charcoal-blue-neutral holding
just above black; the entire *lit* structure comes from the warm 2900K interior
spill through the window. Wet paving does the reflective work.
