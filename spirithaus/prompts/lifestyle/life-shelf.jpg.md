# life-shelf.jpg

**Class:** Lifestyle / retail craft · **Output:** 4096 × 2731 (3:2) · **Safe zone:** none mandated; keep left quarter quiet

## Creative intent
Curation as a discipline. A specialist placing a bottle deliberately — the human
proof behind the word *selection*.

## Composition
Camera at shelf height, slightly off-axis to the right. A hand enters from the
right, fingers around the neck of an unlabelled bottle, mid-placement on an oak
shelf. The shelf run recedes left into bokeh and darkness. Sharp plane on the
knuckles and the bottle neck.

## Hands
Single mature adult hand, 35+. Visible veins on the back, pronounced knuckles,
skin texture and fine lines at the joints, short clean nails, a faint callus at
the base of the fingers. No jewellery. Dark rolled sleeve. **No face.**

## Subject & set
Unlabelled bottle in smoke or amber glass. Burnished oak shelf with scuffed
front edge and visible end-grain. Blank uninked card in a brushed-steel shelf
rail — deliberately empty, no text. Neighbouring unlabelled bottles falling out
of focus to the left.

## Lighting
Key from camera-right at ~100°, medium. Rim on the bottle shoulder and on the
hand's knuckle line. Falloff to near black at the left of frame.
