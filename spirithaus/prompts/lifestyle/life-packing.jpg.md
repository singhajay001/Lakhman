# life-packing.jpg

**Class:** Lifestyle / fulfilment · **Output:** 4096 × 2731 (3:2) · **Safe zone:** none mandated; keep upper-left quadrant quiet

## Creative intent
Care in despatch. The proof that an order is handled by a specialist. Reads as
craft and responsibility, never as gifting or celebration.

## Composition
Three-quarter overhead, ~35° off vertical, onto a worn timber packing bench.
Hands enter from the lower right, mid-action, settling tissue around a bottle in
a kraft box positioned centre-right. Upper-left quadrant falls to shadow.

## Hands
Mature adult, 35+ appearance. Prominent tendons and veins across the back of the
hand, defined knuckles, visible skin texture and dry creases, short unpolished
nails, a small healed nick on one finger. No rings, no watch, no jewellery.
Plain dark rolled sleeve at the wrist. **No face, no arm above the forearm.**

## Subject & set
Unlabelled amber bottle in cream tissue inside a plain kraft box. Jute twine
coil, bare steel box cutter, torn tissue edge. Worn timber bench with deep
gouges and old stains. No printing anywhere.

## Lighting
Key from upper camera-right, medium-hard, modelling the tendons on the back of
the hand and throwing the box shadow left. Amber practical far background.
