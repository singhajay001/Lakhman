# life-build.jpg

**Class:** Lifestyle / preparation ritual · **Output:** 4096 × 2731 (3:2) · **Safe zone:** none mandated; keep upper-left quiet

## Creative intent
Technique, held mid-motion. The stir is the subject — control, patience,
precision. The frame ends before anyone drinks, and shows no finished serve.

## Composition
Camera at bench height, close, slightly above the rim of a cut-crystal mixing
glass positioned centre-right. A hand enters from the right, fingertips guiding
a long bar spoon against the inside wall of the glass. Slight motion blur on the
spoon's twisted stem only; the knuckles stay sharp. Dark walnut recedes left.

## Hands
One mature adult hand, 35+. Veins prominent across the back, knuckles defined,
skin texture visible, short clean nails. Fingers in a correct bar-spoon grip —
spoon held between middle and ring finger, wrist relaxed. No jewellery. Dark
rolled sleeve. **No face.**

## Subject & set
Heavy cut-crystal mixing glass with clear ice and clear spirit, chill haze on
the outside, one bead of condensation running. One unlabelled bottle out of
focus behind. Dark walnut bar top. **No coupe, no finished drink, no second
glass.**

## Lighting
Key from camera-right at ~95°, medium-hard. Crystal facets throw refracted
highlights; the chill haze reads against black. Amber practical deep background.
