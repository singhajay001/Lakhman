# SPIRITHAUS — Visual System

The single source of truth for every campaign frame. Any asset that contradicts
this document is off-brand, regardless of how good it looks in isolation.

## Positioning

> A specialist spirits shop, not a party.

The hero of every frame is one of: the **product category**, the **preparation
ritual**, the **shelf experience**, the **raw ingredient**, the **craft**, or the
**fulfilment process**. Never the drinker, never the occasion.

Reference altitude: Apple, Aesop, Leica, Rimowa. Not conventional alcohol advertising.

## Lighting — the signature

This is what makes the campaign read as one shoot. Do not vary it.

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| Key | Single directional source, 45°–110° off-axis (side or three-quarter rear) |
| Ratio | 6:1 to 8:1 key-to-fill. Deep, committed shadow |
| Quality | Medium-hard. Gridded softbox or bounced tungsten through a 4×4 diffusion frame |
| Colour temp | 2900K–3400K warm tungsten key |
| Fill | Negative fill on the shadow side. Black flags, not white bounce |
| Practicals | Warm amber, always well below key, always motivated |
| Forbidden | Flat frontal light, ring light, bright even catalogue lighting, dual-key beauty setups |

Rim and edge light does the structural work: bottles are read by a bright
shoulder line and a lit meniscus against darkness, not by overall exposure.

## Colour

**Core palette**

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paper / off-white | `#F2EFE9` | Highlight ceiling. Never pure white |
| Near-black | `#111110` | Shadow floor. Never pure black — retain 6–8 IRE detail |
| SPIRITHAUS red | `#CF1C29` | Accent only. One small occurrence per frame, maximum |

**Supporting materials:** warm amber, dark walnut, dark oak, burnished timber,
brushed steel, smoke, stone, charcoal.

**Grade:** filmic. Lifted-but-neutral toe, warm midtones, restrained highlight
rolloff. Saturation held ~85% of native. No teal-and-orange split. No crush.

**Forbidden:** neon, electric blue, bright green, candy colour, any saturated
accent competing with the SPIRITHAUS red.

## Optics

- 50mm, 85mm, or 100mm macro — full-frame look
- T1.4–T2.8 for hero and lifestyle; T4–T5.6 for shelf frames needing depth
- Shallow, honest depth of field with natural falloff and realistic bokeh
- Cinematic focus separation: one plane sharp, everything else surrendered
- Subtle optical imperfection is desirable — mild vignette, real flare, faint
  longitudinal CA on specular edges

## Material realism

Real glass with real internal refraction. Real reflections that agree with the
key. Real condensation that beads and runs, not a uniform speckle. Real timber
grain with real end-grain. Real brushed metal with anisotropic streak. Real dust,
real fingerprints on steel, real scuffs on a shelf edge.

Photographic authenticity is non-negotiable. Nothing may read as CGI, 3D render,
or illustration.

## Typography-safe zones

| Asset class | Reserved zone | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Hero banners | Entire **left third** | Empty, dark, quiet. No objects, no specular highlights, no clutter. Subject sits right of centre |
| Homepage tiles | **Centre third** | Empty and low-contrast. Visual interest lives at the edges |
| Lifestyle | None mandated | Still compose for a caption; keep one quiet quadrant |

Safe zones are structural, not decorative. A hero with a highlight in the left
third is a failed frame and gets rejected, not retouched.

## Human depiction

Hands only, and only where the ritual requires them.

**Required:** mature adult, 35+ appearance. Visible veins, visible knuckles,
visible skin texture, authentic wear, short clean nails, plausible working hands.

**Forbidden:** faces, any part of a face, young-looking hands, fashion-model
hands, smooth or retouched skin, ambiguous age, jewellery that reads as styling.

## Responsible alcohol — hard constraints

Never generate: drinking, glass touching lips, consumption, celebration,
parties, crowds, nightclubs, romantic scenes, social-success or business-success
messaging, intoxication, empty bottles, drained glasses, multiple served drinks,
rows of shots, or excess quantity.

Never imply that alcohol produces popularity, confidence, status, success,
attraction, or social acceptance.

Serve-ware rule: at most **one** prepared drink in frame, always untouched,
always presented as the outcome of craft rather than an invitation to consume.

## Content restrictions

**Never:** faces · readable labels · logos · brand names · watermarks · text ·
typography · advertising copy · real product labels · invented product branding.

**Never:** beaches · boats · water sports · swimming pools · motor vehicles ·
machinery · sporting environments.

**Never:** cartoon · illustration · anime · CGI · 3D-render appearance.

Bottles appear **unlabelled** — bare glass, blind embossing, wax, foil, or a
plain uninked paper band. This is a deliberate campaign device, not an omission.

## Technical output

Photorealistic · cinematic · commercially usable · luxury advertising quality ·
high-end editorial · print-quality · 4K minimum, 8K preferred · premium colour
grade · sharp but natural · delivered optimised for web and print.

Delivery: sRGB JPEG quality 88–92 for web; retain a 16-bit master. Sharpen for
output medium last, never in the master.
