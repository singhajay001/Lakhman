# Shared Style Block

Two reusable strings. Every asset spec in `prompts/` references these rather
than restating them, which is what keeps the campaign visually continuous.

## STYLE (append to every positive prompt)

```
Shot as premium commercial advertising photography on a full-frame cinema
camera. Single directional warm tungsten key at roughly 3200K raking from the
side and slightly behind, 7:1 key-to-fill ratio, negative fill on the shadow
side, deep committed shadows, no frontal light. Low-key cinematic exposure with
a near-black #111110 shadow floor that still holds detail and an off-white
#F2EFE9 highlight ceiling. Palette restricted to warm amber, dark walnut, dark
oak, burnished timber, brushed steel, smoke, stone and charcoal. Shallow depth
of field with natural falloff and realistic bokeh, cinematic focus separation.
Real glass with true refraction, real reflections, real condensation, real
timber grain, real brushed metal, authentic surface imperfection, visible dust
and micro-scratches. Filmic grade, restrained saturation, gentle highlight
rolloff, subtle vignette, fine natural grain. Photorealistic, print quality,
8K, luxury editorial advertising.
```
