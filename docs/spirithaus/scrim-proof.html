What the check proves. Three rules, measured on the composited frame at every ratio the theme renders, not just the one on screen. Type contrast is WCAG luminance for white type against the brightest patch of the quiet zone, not its average — one specular highlight breaks a headline and a mean hides it. Zone intrusion is the peak level above the zone's own floor, which catches an object or edge entering the band that must stay empty; at a 58–66% scrim almost any patch clears the contrast bar, so this is the check that does the work. Image survival reads the brightest tenth of the subject side, because low-key work is meant to have a dark mean and what says the photograph is still alive is whether its highlights survive.

What the fix does, and does not. It slides the square master window across the source to find the crop with the cleanest quiet zone, then veils the lower band of that master in ink at the lowest strength that passes every ratio, capped at 55% so a frame can never be “fixed” by turning the band into a black bar. Both are real edits to the pixels you export. It cannot add pixels that were never shot: a 3:1 source has nothing above and below to build a square from, so the best it can do is report that and leave the reshoot to you.

What neither can tell you. Whether a hand reads as 35 or as 20, and every other line of the ABAC list. Those stay a human pass before anything ships.

Where the numbers come from. Ratios, scrim values and quiet zones are measured from the live theme (sections/spirithaus-hero.liquid, sections/spirithaus-categories.liquid, assets/spirithaus.css) as at 2026-09-24. If the theme changes, re-read it and update the prompt pack and this page together.
