# Spirithaus — supplier stockist link outreach

**Date:** 2026-09-26. Spirithaus only.
**Goal:** earn the first genuine backlinks to `spirithaus.com.au`. Discovery, not
technical SEO, is what is holding indexation back — the site is now technically
sound and has 238 live pages, but almost nothing on the web links to it.

## ⚠️ Send these from a Spirithaus address

The email on file for this workspace is
`info@trafalgarsupermarketandcellars.com.au`. **Do not send Spirithaus outreach
from it.** Two reasons:

1. It merges the two businesses' identities, which is the one thing CLAUDE.md
   rules out. A distiller who files that contact against Spirithaus creates a
   citation tying the two together, and NAP inconsistency is very hard to unpick
   later.
2. It reads wrong. A distiller checking whether Spirithaus is a real account
   sees a different business's domain in the From line.

Use a `@spirithaus.com.au` address throughout, and the Spirithaus licence
number, not Trafalgar's.

## Why this works and paid links do not

These are the highest-quality links available to a new liquor retailer:

- **Topically exact.** A gin distillery linking to a gin retailer is the
  relationship Google's link graph is built to reward.
- **Editorially given.** You are not buying them; the distiller lists you
  because you genuinely stock them. That is the only kind of link worth having.
- **Permanent.** Stockist pages are maintained, not rotated out.
- **Free**, and you already have the commercial relationship.

## What to expect, honestly

Not every one will say yes, and the ones that do will take a few weeks. A
realistic outcome from thirteen approaches is **four to six links**. That is a
good result — it is roughly four to six more than the site has now, and they
are the right kind.

The first link matters far more than the fifth: it is what gives Googlebot a
reason to crawl the site at all rather than relying on the sitemap alone.
