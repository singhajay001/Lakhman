## What this export does and does not close

**Closes:** the 19 barcodes above, plus cost and pack data for the spirits range
generally — which is what the 20% margin floor is checked against.

**Does not close:** the 114 unpriced fine-wine drafts. Those are Penfolds Grange,
Krug, Dom Pérignon and similar. ALM is a broad-range wholesaler but allocated
prestige wine of that kind is normally bought direct from the producer or through
a fine-wine merchant, not off a general liquor wholesaler's list. Expect those to
need a separate source. If a few do appear in the ALM file, that's a bonus, not
the plan.

## A note on which business is asking

The ALM account and the POS export both sit on the Trafalgar side. The product
data is being used to populate the Spirithaus catalogue. That is fine — same
owner, and barcodes are manufacturer data, not anything business-specific.

But send this from whichever account actually holds the ALM login, and do not
mention Spirithaus in it. The two businesses are kept deliberately separate, and
there is no reason to introduce a link between them in a supplier's records.
