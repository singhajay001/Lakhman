**Scope:** Spirithaus only.
**Source rule (owner's decision, this session):** ALM landed list cost only.
Trafalgar's POS costs were **not** used — the two businesses stay separate.

## What "not in ALM" turned out to mean

The first pass matched 28 and left 152. That 152 was not 152 sourcing problems:

- **115 are drafts** — the fine-wine wishlist. These sit in the `3xxxxxxx`
  specialty series, which the ALM extract does not cover (it runs `00002243`–`00999254`).
  Not a problem: they are not for sale, and they are priced off the fine-wine
  quote sheet, not off this extract.
- **17 were a matching failure, not a sourcing failure** — ALM carries the exact
  line, the first pass just failed to find it. Now written.
- **20 are live with genuinely no ALM line.** This is the real list.

good deal price leaves very little in it.

## Follow-up fixes applied

### 1800 Coconut — the 50ml was wrong in two places

The SKU said 50ml and so did the shipping weight. Both came from the same wrong
assumption. Every sibling 700ml in the range (Silver, Reposado, Añejo,
Cristalino, Jose Cuervo Especial Silver) carries 1.25 kg; Coconut alone carried
0.25 kg. ALM does not list a 50ml at all — only 700ml ($68.33) and 200ml
($21.10) — and the $79.99 shelf price is a 700ml price against ALM's $92.99 RRP.

| Field | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| SKU | `SH-TEQ-1800-COCONUT-50` | `SH-TEQ-1800-COCONUT-700` |
| Weight | 0.25 kg | 1.25 kg |
| Cost | none | $68.33 |
| Barcode | none | `7501035013742` |

The barcode is the consumer (G-type) APN on ALM item `00276211`
(1800 COCONUT TEQUILA 700ML) — read straight from the extract, not derived.

The wrong weight was quietly overcharging or undercharging shipping on every
order containing this product, and the wrong SKU would have carried into any
stocktake or supplier order.

### Billecart-Salmon Brut Rosé — priced at $217.99

Cost $162.79. Priced on the same convention as the rest of the fine-wine range:
25% GP figured on the shelf price. $217.99 gives 25.3%, matching Penfolds Grange
(25.0%) and St Henri (25.3%).

Note that convention computes GP on the **GST-inclusive** price. Ex-GST the real
margin is nearer 18%. That is how all 143 fine-wine drafts were priced, so
Billecart is consistent with them — but if the 25% floor was meant as a true
margin, the whole fine-wine sheet is set about 7 points light and wants revisiting
as one job, not product by product.

## Still open: 94 fine-wine drafts priced at $0.00

Billecart was not alone — 94 other draft fine wines also sit at $0.00. It was the
only one that could be priced, because it was the only one with a cost.
**None of the other 94 has a cost**, so none can be priced yet. They are blocked
on the same thing as before: the fine-wine `3xxxxxxx` series is outside the ALM
extract's range, so their costs have to come off the fine-wine quote sheet.

They are all drafts, so nothing is exposed to customers. But 94 products cannot
go live until those costs land.
