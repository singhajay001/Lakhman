## What "not in ALM" turned out to mean

The first pass matched 28 and left 152. That 152 was not 152 sourcing problems:

- **115 are drafts** — the fine-wine wishlist. These sit in the `3xxxxxxx`
  specialty series, which the ALM extract does not cover (it runs `00002243`–`00999254`).
  Not a problem: they are not for sale, and they are priced off the fine-wine
  quote sheet, not off this extract.
- **17 were a matching failure, not a sourcing failure** — ALM carries the exact
  line, the first pass just failed to find it. Now written.
- **20 are live with genuinely no ALM line.** This is the real list.

**Read this as a ceiling on cost, not a measurement of margin.** ALM list cost is
what a one-carton buyer pays before deal, rebate and volume support. Where actual
invoice cost has been checked it has come in materially lower — Bacardi was
$33.41 actual against $49.36 ALM list, Laphroaig 37.1% actual against 17.2% list,
Monkey Shoulder 18.2% against 7.9%. So most of this table is understating margin.

The three below cost still need looking at, because list cost is a ceiling and a
negative number against the ceiling means the real margin is thin at best. Four
Pillars Navy Strength at $99.99 against a $103.37 list is the sharpest: even a
good deal price leaves very little in it.
