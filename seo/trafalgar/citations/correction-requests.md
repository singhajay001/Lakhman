## Notes

**Send these yourself.** They are drafts for you to review and send from your own
address — I have no ability to email on your behalf, and a correction request
should come from the owner.

**Expect slow, partial responses.** Small directories often ignore email; the
"suggest an edit" form usually works better. Chase once after two weeks, then let
it go — a stale low-authority listing matters far less than Google, the banner
site, and the two liquor directories.

**Do not create new listings** on directories where you are not already present,
as a way of "fixing" things. Volume is not the goal; agreement is.
