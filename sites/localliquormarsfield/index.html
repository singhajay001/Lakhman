Local Liquor Marsfield

The bottle shop at Trafalgar Place, Marsfield. Beer, wine and spirits, a short walk from home — open from 8am, and until 10pm Thursday to Saturday.

We open at 10am on Sundays. Packaged liquor trading hours on Sunday mornings are set by NSW law, so this is later than the supermarket next door, which opens at 8am seven days.

Next to the supermarket

We are in the same complex as Trafalgar Supermarket and Cellars, in Shop 5A. Do your groceries and pick up a bottle in the one stop.

Serving the area

Marsfield, Macquarie Park, North Ryde, Eastwood and Epping — and anyone at Macquarie University looking for something closer than the shopping centre.

What we stock

Over 450 craft beers, more than 2,100 wines and 1,400 spirits — a deeper range than a shop this size usually carries. Australian wine sorted by region, a growing gin and whisky shelf, and cold beer ready to walk out with.
