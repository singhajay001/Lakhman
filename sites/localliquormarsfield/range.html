Our range

A bigger range than a shop this size has any right to carry — over 450 craft beers, more than 2,100 wines and 1,400 spirits, from bottles under $15 to cellar releases. If it is not on the shelf, ask us and we will usually get it in.

Beer & cider

Just over a thousand lines across the fridges and shelves — and the craft range is the part we are proudest of.

Craft beer — 450+

Independent Australian brewers alongside imports. This is a deeper craft range than most bottle shops our size carry, and it is where we can beat a chain outright.

Australian & imported

Around 270 Australian lines and 120 imports, in cartons, six-packs and singles. The everyday favourites are always cold.

Cider — 130+

Apple, pear and fruit ciders, Australian and imported, sweet through dry.

Low carb, mid strength & zero

Around 50 lines across low-carb, mid-strength and non-alcoholic beer, for whoever is driving.

Wine

Over 2,100 wines — the largest part of the shop, and organised so you can actually find a region rather than hunting a wall of labels.

Red — 1,100+

Shiraz, cabernet, pinot, grenache and blends. Barossa, McLaren Vale, Coonawarra, Yarra Valley and Margaret River all well represented.

White — 650+

Sauvignon blanc, chardonnay, riesling and pinot gris, from Marlborough, Adelaide Hills, Clare Valley and the Mornington Peninsula.

Champagne & sparkling — 290

Prosecco and Australian sparkling through to Champagne proper.

Fortified & cask

Port, sherry, muscat and tawny, plus cask wine in the usual sizes.

Spirits

Around 1,400 lines, from everyday pouring bottles to single malts worth saving.

Whisky — 250+

Scotch by region — Speyside, Highland, Islay — alongside Australian, Irish, American and Japanese whisky.

Gin — 180+

London dry through to Australian botanical gins. One of the fastest-growing shelves in the shop.

Vodka, rum & tequila

Around 220 lines between them, including agave tequila and spiced rum.

Brandy, cognac & liqueurs

Roughly 160 lines, from cooking brandy to aged cognac.

Premixed — 600+

Cans and bottles ready to go, including canned cocktails. The biggest single sub-range in the shop.

Award winners

More than a thousand of the bottles we stock have won something — medals at the International Spirits Challenge, the World Cocktail Awards, Australian wine shows. If you want a safe bet for a gift or a dinner, ask which of them we have in, and at what price.

Can’t see what you want?

Ring (02) 9868 1070. We will check the shelf while you are on the phone, and if we do not have it we can usually order it in.
