About us

Your neighbourhood bottle shop in the heart of Marsfield. A bottle shop has stood on this site since 1984.

We stock an extensive range of beer, wine, spirits, RTDs, mixers and non-alcoholic options — from the everyday brands people actually buy, to independent NSW makers and imported labels you will not find in a chain store. Everything is priced fairly, and the fridges are cold seven days a week.

Our focus is simple: a genuine range, honest prices, cold stock ready when you are, and a team that knows what it is selling.

We are proud to serve the Marsfield, Macquarie Park, North Ryde, Eastwood, Epping and wider Ryde community. Whether you are grabbing a cold six-pack on the way home, choosing a bottle for dinner or stocking up for an occasion, Local Liquor Marsfield is here for you, seven days a week.

Our story

Liquor retailing on this site goes back a long way. A bottle shop has stood here since 1984, serving generations of local families, students and professionals, and building a reputation for range, value and genuine community connection.

Under new management, we have brought fresh energy and a renewed focus to the bottle shop — while keeping everything that makes a good local worth walking to. We know our regulars by name, we listen to what they ask for, and we work hard every day to make this the bottle shop Marsfield recommends.

Since taking over we have significantly expanded the range: more independent and craft beer, a deeper wine selection, a growing spirits wall, a full zero-alcohol offer and a much wider ready-to-drink range. We have partnered directly with local producers including Karu Distillery and North Wall Kombucha, and we add new products every month based on what our customers ask for.

For us this is more than a bottle shop. It is a local business built around the community it serves, and that community is at the heart of every decision we make.

Our mission

To be the bottle shop our community actually recommends — real range, honest prices, cold stock every day, and advice from people who know what they are talking about.

We are committed to:

  • Offering a genuine range — depth and choice across beer, wine, spirits, RTDs and non-alcoholic options
  • Pricing fairly and transparently, with clear ticketing and no gimmicks
  • Keeping stock cold and ready to go, every day we are open
  • Supporting Australian and NSW independent brewers, distillers and winemakers
  • Giving honest, knowledgeable advice — the right bottle for the occasion and the budget, not the most expensive one
  • Continuously expanding our range based on what our customers ask for
  • Making every visit fast, friendly and worth the walk
  • The responsible service of alcohol under NSW liquor law, including checking identification and refusing service where required

Part of Trafalgar Supermarket and Cellars

Local Liquor Marsfield and Trafalgar Supermarket and Cellars are run by the same family business in the same complex at 1 Trafalgar Place. The supermarket is in Shop 5 and trades from 8am daily; the bottle shop is in Shop 5A. Groceries and a bottle in the one trip.

Responsible service of alcohol

We are licensed under the Liquor Act 2007 (NSW). It is against the law to sell or supply alcohol to, or to obtain alcohol on behalf of, a person under 18 years of age. We ask for ID, and we refuse service where we are required to.
