About Local Liquor Marsfield

We are the bottle shop at Trafalgar Place in Marsfield, next door to Trafalgar Supermarket and Cellars.

Part of Trafalgar Supermarket and Cellars

Local Liquor Marsfield and Trafalgar Supermarket and Cellars are run by the same family business in the same complex at 1 Trafalgar Place. The supermarket is in Shop 5 and trades 8am daily; the bottle shop is in Shop 5A. Groceries and a bottle in the one trip.

Serving Marsfield and around

Most of our customers walk or drive from Marsfield, Macquarie Park, North Ryde, Eastwood and Epping. We are the close option — no shopping centre car park, no queue, and someone behind the counter who knows the shelves.

Responsible service of alcohol

We are licensed under the Liquor Act 2007 (NSW). It is against the law to sell or supply alcohol to, or to obtain alcohol on behalf of, a person under 18 years of age. We ask for ID, and we refuse service where we are required to.
